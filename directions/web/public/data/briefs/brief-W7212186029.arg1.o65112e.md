## Objection

A theologian maintains a three-valued parthood catalogue. It records omniscience as part of God, while whether God is part of omniscience is explicitly undetermined. A proper part is defined as a part whose reverse parthood is false, not merely unconfirmed. Omniscience is therefore not a proper part of God, and the catalogue contains no other candidate parts, so “God has no proper parts” is true. Yet “God is part of all God’s parts” is not true, because God’s parthood of omniscience remains undetermined. The absence of asymmetric parthood does not establish reciprocal parthood when undetermined relations are permitted. Premise W7212186029.c008 fails because having no proper parts does not yield symmetry when reverse parthood can be neither true nor false.
