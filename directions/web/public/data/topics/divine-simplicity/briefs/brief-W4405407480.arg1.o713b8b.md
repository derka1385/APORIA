## Challenged premise
**W4405407480.c014**: The truth of the proposition ‘God freely chooses to create the actual world’ depends on the conjunction of a necessary fact about God and a contingent fact about the world.

## Objection
*reasoner:minimalist · qwen:Qwen/Qwen3.8-27B*

The premise assumes that the truth of 'God freely chooses to create the actual world' requires a contingent fact about the world as a distinct truthmaking component alongside a necessary fact about God. But the smuggled assumption is that this proposition is not wholly a truth about God. It is: it asserts something about God's act of choosing. The premise needs the contingent world-fact to make the truthmaker extrinsic (feeding c013 and c015), yet c012 states that all non-tautological truths only about God have only God as their minimal truthmaker. The proposition 'God freely chooses to create the actual world' is a truth only about God—it predicates a choice of God. To split its truthmaker into a God-component and a world-component is to treat it as a truth partly about the world, which contradicts its grammatical and metaphysical subject. The argument cannot have the contingent world-fact as a truthmaker for this proposition without violating c012, yet it needs that contingent fact to generate the extrinsic relation in c013 and c015. The conjunction is incoherent within the framework the argument itself sets.

## Strongest responses and why they failed
- **Defender A**: The argument should be revised so that God alone is the minimal truthmaker for the choice-proposition, with the contingent world-fact entering only as the content of the choice rather than as a distinct truthmaking component, and c013 and c015 should be restated in terms of extrinsic content rather than extrinsic truthmakers. — *It conceded the original inconsistency between c012 and c014 and offered a substantive repair that rejects c014's conjunctive truthmaker rather than reinterpreting c012, but the exchange did not test whether extrinsic content can perform the modal work originally assigned to extrinsic truthmakers without reintroducing collapse.*
- **Defender B**: Divine simplicity requires only that non-tautological non-relational truths only about God have God alone as their minimal truthmaker, so c012 does not govern the relational truth in c014, whose truthmaker naturally includes the world as relatum. — *The objector showed that the world is not a pre-existing co-participant but the product of God's act, making the proposition a truth only about God, and that simply stipulating the framework allows extrinsic relations without undermining simplicity assumes the conclusion the argument must earn; Defender B ultimately conceded the need for an explicit non-relational restriction but did not vindicate it against the charge of ad hocery or show how c014's conjunction yields an extrinsic property without circularity.*
