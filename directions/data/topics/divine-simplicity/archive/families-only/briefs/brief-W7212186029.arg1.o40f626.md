## Challenged premise

## Objection
*tradition_lens · gpt-oss:openai/gpt-oss-120b*

A community of believers holds that God's wisdom, power, and love are parts of God, and they argue that this is viable because instantiation is modelled as parthood. In truthmaker theory each true statement must be grounded by a concrete reality that makes it true. The statement "God's wisdom is a part of God" would need a truthmaker: an entity that is both God and the particular instantiation of wisdom. Since God's attributes are not distinct entities but modes of a simple being, there is no separate grounding fact that can serve as a truthmaker for the parthood claim. Thus the purported parthood relation is truthmaker‑orphaned, showing the premise that such modelling makes the symmetry formulation viable is false.
