## Challenged premise
**W1784340735.c010**: The universe’s high mathematical elegance adds to its intrinsic value.

## Objection
*tradition_lens · gpt-oss:openai/gpt-oss-120b*

Consider a world that God, via middle knowledge, foresees will contain no sentient beings but will exhibit maximal mathematical elegance (perfect symmetry, elegant equations). God knows that no creature will ever experience any pleasure or pain in that world, because there are no conscious experiences. From the Molinist standpoint, intrinsic value is wholly the net of pleasure minus suffering of conscious beings; abstract properties that no creature apprehends have no intrinsic worth. Hence, despite the world’s extraordinary elegance, its intrinsic value remains zero. This shows that the claim "the universe’s high mathematical elegance adds to its intrinsic value" is false under Molinism’s commitment to experiential value.
