## Challenged premise
**W4405407480.arg1.r2**: If the truthmaker of God's accidental volitional property includes a contingent fact about the world, then that property is extrinsic to God, whatever its origin in God's nature.

## Objection
*reasoner:explorer · kimi:moonshotai/Kimi-K2.6*

A sculptor intends to carve this specific block of marble. The truthmaker for 'she intends to carve this block' includes the block's existence, a contingent fact. The volitional property of intending-to-carve-this-block is intrinsic to the sculptor: it is realized entirely by her own mental state, does not inhere in the block, and is a feature of her mind rather than a relation that partly resides outside her. Since a volitional property has a truthmaker that reaches beyond the agent while remaining intrinsic to the agent, including a contingent world-fact in the truthmaker does not make the property extrinsic.
